# Michael Cebral Journey Film — Production Plan (Revision B)

**Date:** 2026-06-18. Supersedes the in-engine map approach. Transitions are now generated Higgsfield videos. No gold thread anywhere.

## 1. Overview, the new structure

One continuous cinematic journey from Lugo to ESTEC, built from **eleven clips**: six city clips and five generated transition videos in the seams. Pure hyperrealistic cinematic film, maximum realism, filmic grade, slow deliberate camera, empty of people.

```
[Lugo] -> T1 -> [Santiago] -> T2 -> [London] -> T3 -> [Stockholm] -> T4 -> [Strasbourg] -> T5 -> [ESTEC]
```

**Key technique:** every transition is a `seedance_2_0` clip generated with a start image AND an end image. The start image is the **last frame** of the city before it, the end image is the **first frame** of the city after it. Seedance interpolates between the two along a prompt that flies or morphs between the two landscapes, blends both countries' characteristics, and carries one beat of Michael's story. Because each transition begins on the previous clip's final frame and ends on the next clip's opening frame, the eleven clips concatenate into one unbroken film, no visible joins, no thread, no map graphic, no text.

City clips: a `nano_banana_2` still (regrade the real reference, weave in characteristics + a quiet nod to his work) then a `seedance_2_0` motion clip (8 to 10s, 1080p, 24fps). Transitions skip the still and use the two city frames directly.

The science lives in the light: one tall ground floor lab window glows warm clinical white, the lonely note of a young researcher abroad.
- **Stockholm, the fusion climax.** The Aula Medica facade graded so the panes read as a tissue map and a star field at once, his NASA Rodent Research 9 spatial transcriptomics. His recombinant spider silk enters as one frost laden gossamer filament on a birch twig in the snow, life turned to thread. Snow, frost, long cold blue shadows. Biology and space become the same thing.
- **Strasbourg, the pivot and liftoff.** The ISU campus above its lake at Alsace dusk, a satellite dome catching the last light. His ESA Academy parabolic flight (GARN, weightlessness) reads as the camera losing gravity and drifting up. ROADMAP, his space communication platform (IAC 2026), reads as the dome and the opening sky. The bench leaves the ground.
- **ESTEC, the destination.** The real campus on the flat Dutch coast, dunes of marram grass, the North Sea at golden hour. As incoming Science Communication Intern at the Exploration Science Office his job is to send the science outward, so one slender antenna catches the light and points to sea. The frame lifts the eye out over the water, holding the rise to orbit and the implicit curve home to Galicia.

## 4. Assembly into the scroll engine

Eleven segments, each a first class scroll segment with its own `framesDir`:

```
seg01 lugo       seg02 t1   seg03 santiago  seg04 t2  seg05 london  seg06 t3
seg07 stockholm  seg08 t4   seg09 strasbourg seg10 t5  seg11 estec
```

City segments get a longer scroll allocation (dwell beats), transitions a shorter one (connective travel); every segment is scrubbed the same way.

**Boundary frame rule (load-bearing for one continuous take):** after each city clip is extracted at 24fps, export its true **last** frame and true **first** frame as `XX-city_last.png` / `XX-city_first.png`. These exact PNGs feed the neighbouring transition seedance jobs as `--start-image` and `--end-image`. Do not regrade or crop them. Seedance's first output frame equals the start image and its last equals the end image, so the transition begins on the exact pixel state where city A ended and ends where city B begins. Drop the duplicated boundary frame at each of the ten joins so it is not held twice.
