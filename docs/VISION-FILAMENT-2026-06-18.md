## The one big idea

One dark editorial site where a single champagne gold hairline (#D4AF37) is the spine of everything. The same unbroken filament that is **spider silk** at the cellular scale is drawn, never severed, into an **orbital path** and then the **coastline of Galicia**. It physically connects the home, the story, the CV and the projects, so the whole site reads as one continuous act of mapping rather than separate pages.

The recurring interaction across every surface is a **focal ring** that zooms the same view from petri dish to planet without a cut. CELL at one end, PLANET at the other. The throughline made operable.

The recurring texture is a scatter of fine gold points that is **simultaneously a spatial transcriptomics tissue slice and a star field**. The deliberate ambiguity is the entire point of him: biology and cosmos in the same image.

Proof is always real and named, with no inflation. A **polymath ledger** gives sport, kitchen, languages and music equal museum billing. The existing playful depth (the alien, the hidden Space Invaders game) is woven into the filament motif as canon, not bolted on. Every orbit returns to the same coast. Lugo holds the other end of the thread.

**Identity thesis:** *Michael runs the same instrument at two scales. He reads the code of life in a petri dish and the code of nations in orbit, and he carries both home to Lugo.*

Tone: Rigorous. Rooted. Cosmic. Understated. Playful.

- Press and hold the gold filament: it plays a faint plucked string note (audio opt-in, silent by default). Tug it three times for the line *it refuses to snap*. On a full pull it spells **cose** (Galician for "it sews").
- Hover the empanada in the ledger for a puff of steam and the line *forno aberto*; click it for one line of Galician about Lugo.
- Hover the GL / ES / EN ticks and the bookend lines retype in Galician, English and Spanish.
- Drag the focal ring past the cosmos end and the star field briefly rearranges into the ALL IN SPACE globe. Hold past the planet and the wandering alien grabs the thread end like a kite string.
